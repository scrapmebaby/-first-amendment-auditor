Dialogue research notes — v0.10 (2026-10-07)

The game's exchanges are original fictional satire. No transcripts, audio,
real names, or identifiable encounters are reproduced in the game. Sources
were used to identify recurring situations, not as a dialogue dataset or as
legal authority. No network service or AI generation runs while playing.

1. "Who Hired You?" — HonorYourOath, third-party transcript indexed by
   1A Intelligence Desk:
   https://1a.auditorintel.com/?video_id=WauSOXkhG3Y&view=transcript
   The indexed exchange illustrates disagreement over public sidewalks versus
   business frontage, suspicions about motives, and police mediation.
   The transcript has apparent recognition errors; its speaker assignments and
   legal statements were not independently verified against the original video.

2. "Assault on Camera: I Got You Hitting Me!" — CT3 compilation, third-party
   transcript indexed by 1A Intelligence Desk:
   https://1a.auditorintel.com/?video_id=Pt6Vg7GSnRM&view=transcript
   The opening excerpt contains disputes about identification, security and
   demands to cite a law. It includes commentary and possible transcription
   errors. Only recurring conversational themes informed this game's writing.

3. American Library Association, "Auditing the First Amendment at Your Public
   Library" (2019):
   https://www.oif.ala.org/auditing-the-first-amendment-at-your-public-library/
   Background on library patrons' privacy, filming and interruptions. This is
   institutional guidance, not an encounter transcript. It informed the library
   setting and the distinction between staff, patrons and private information.

Implementation: 41 authored four-turn exchanges (164 lines), plus the existing
200 event-driven lines. Context chooses library, cafe, civic, street, rain,
recognized or heated pools. Shuffled bags avoid reuse until a pool is exhausted.
Each exchange stays paired so replies answer the preceding line. Real gameplay
reactions (leaving, music, crying, police, violence) can interrupt the banter.
